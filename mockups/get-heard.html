Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Submitting a set is free. Our paid services are optional and have no effect on who we recommend.

Will I definitely get a gig?

No one can promise that. We pass on the sets we believe in, and the venue decides.

How long until I hear back?

Usually within two weeks. If your set is a fit for a specific venue, we'll ask you before sharing it.
