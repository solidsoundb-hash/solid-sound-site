How to Get Booked at Clubs and Festivals

Behind every line-up there's a person with an inbox full of links. Most of them never get opened. This guide shows you who books, when they book and how to write the message that does get opened.

Quick answer: find the person who books (promoter, talent buyer or resident), contact them 2–4 months before the season, send a short, personal message with one set link and your press kit, then follow up once after two weeks.

👤Who actually books

VenueWho decidesBest way in
Bars and small clubsOwner or night promoterGo to the night, talk in person
Big clubsTalent buyer, promoters of each nightResidents and warm-up slots first
Seasonal venues (beach clubs, resorts, film festival parties)Venue managerContact well before the season starts
FestivalsProgrammers or stage hostsOpen calls, local stages, label showcases

📅When to reach out

Line-ups are built long before the night. For seasonal venues and festivals, think 2–6 months ahead: summer seasons are booked in winter and spring, and parties around big spring events are planned months before.

✉The message that gets answered

Hi Marie, I came to your Friday night at the harbour club last month. The 2am melodic set was exactly my sound. I'm a Paris-based DJ (melodic techno, 2 years of residencies). Here's a 60-minute set recorded last week: [link]. My one-page press kit: [link]. I'd love to play a warm-up slot this season. Thanks for listening!
  • Personal: show you know their night.
  • Short: under 100 words.
  • One link to your best set, no attachments.
  • A clear ask: a warm-up slot, not "any gig".

✓Before you hit send

  • The set link opens without a login
  • The first 30 seconds of your set are strong
  • Your mix is clean and loud enough
  • Press kit link works on a phone
  • Reminder set for one follow-up in two weeks

?FAQ

Should I pay to play?

No. Paying for a slot or being forced to sell tickets in advance rarely leads anywhere. Real promoters book for the music and the crowd.

What if they don't answer?

Follow up once after two weeks. If there's still no answer, move on and try again next season with a new set.
