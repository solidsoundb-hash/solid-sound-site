Hey all. A lot of people ask whether you can get a club-ready dark techno sound without third-party plugins. You can. Quick breakdown of what I do:

1. Kick: Operator, one sine carrier, fast pitch envelope (~40 ms), a touch of Saturator, then Glue Compressor on the drum bus.
