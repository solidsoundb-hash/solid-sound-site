Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

We want you to be happy with every purchase. This policy sits alongside our Terms of Sale. Nothing here limits your statutory rights as a consumer.

Digital products (templates, presets, sample packs, music)

EU consumers normally have 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase. For digital content, this right ends once the download is made available, because at checkout you expressly ask us to deliver it immediately and confirm that you lose the right of withdrawal. Without that confirmation we do not complete the order.

This does not affect your right to a working product. If a file is damaged, incomplete, does not match its description, or you did not receive it, contact us within 30 days and we will fix it, replace it or refund you.

Please check the requirements on the product page (DAW and version, third-party plugins) before you buy. Refunds are not available if a product works as described but your setup does not meet the listed requirements.

Studio services (mixing, mastering, production, sound design)
You can cancel within 14 days of ordering for a full refund if we have not started work.
If you asked us to start earlier and then cancel, we refund the price minus the part of the work already done.
Once the service is fully delivered with your prior express agreement, the right of withdrawal no longer applies. Revisions are included as stated on the service page.
How to ask

To cancel an order online, use our Cancel an order form. We confirm receipt by e-mail right away.

Refunds are made in euros (EUR), the currency of your order. If your bank converted the payment, the amount on your statement may differ slightly because of exchange rates.
