FAQ

Questions couples ask

Why an iPhone and not a big camera?

It records 4K video and 48 MP photos, and it's made for vertical content. Guests behave naturally around a phone, so moments stay real. And it lets me edit and send your teaser the same night.

We already have a photographer. Can you work together?

Yes, that's a common setup. Your photographer takes the classic photos, I focus on reels and behind-the-scenes. I agree the plan with them before the day.

What happens if the weather is bad or something breaks?

I bring backup batteries, a second phone and extra storage, and copy footage during the day. For rain we plan indoor spots in advance.

How do we receive everything?

Through a private online gallery you can download from and share with family. Reels come in full quality, ready to post.

Can you travel?

Yes, anywhere in France and abroad. Up to 50 km is included, beyond that it's 0,40 € per km, plus accommodation for destination weddings.
